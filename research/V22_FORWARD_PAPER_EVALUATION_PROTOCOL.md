# v2.2 Future-Only Paper Evaluation Protocol

## Status

Pre-registered before evaluator implementation and before any v2.1 decision is scored against a later price.

## Safety and claim boundary

- Paper research only.
- No order routing, wallets, credentials, leverage, futures, shorts or live execution.
- This evaluator may authorize only a later time-limited shadow-paper candidate.
- It can never authorize real-money trading.
- Historical OHLCV windows used by retired families are prohibited.

## Immutable inputs

Only append-only records from `forward-data/v2` may be used:

1. normalized v2.0 market-state snapshots; and
2. normalized v2.1 router decisions and their inventories.

Every input record hash and report hash must verify. Duplicate hourly records must be byte-identical or evaluation fails closed.

## Activation and no-peeking rule

Activation is the first v2.1 decision that:

- contains 169 contiguous forward snapshots;
- has a corresponding v2.0 snapshot in the following UTC hour; and
- was generated by the frozen v2.1 protocol and implementation fingerprints.

Before a complete discovery block exists, the evaluator may report only:

- activation status;
- available contiguous decision/fill hours;
- missing hours; and
- immutable input fingerprints.

It must not calculate or expose partial return, drawdown, trade, sleeve or benchmark performance.

## Frozen evaluation timeline

After activation:

- Discovery block: exactly 2,160 consecutive hourly decision-to-fill intervals (90 days).
- Discovery first half: intervals 1–1,080.
- Discovery second half: intervals 1,081–2,160.
- Promotion holdout: the following 720 intervals (30 days).
- The holdout must not be loaded, priced or summarized unless the complete discovery gate passes.
- Any missing decision hour, missing next-hour snapshot or invalid hash prevents the block from being complete.

The first complete activation fixes these boundaries permanently. Later files cannot move the start date.

## Paper portfolio and execution

- Starting deployable capital: INR 100,000 equivalent.
- One shared long-or-cash wallet.
- Router target weights are applied at the earliest valid v2.0 spot mid in the following UTC hour.
- Missing router decision means target cash for that hour.
- Missing next-hour spot mid means the interval is incomplete; no synthetic fill is permitted.
- Holdings are marked at each valid hourly spot mid.
- Rebalance only to the exact frozen router target weights.
- No leverage, shorting, borrowing or fractional cash creation.
- Buy quantity is limited by deployable cash after estimated buy costs.
- Sales use proportional average cost basis, including proportional acquisition fees and slippage.

## Frozen costs and Indian crypto tax estimates

Base execution assumptions, charged on every actual transaction:

- Exchange fee: 0.10% of transaction consideration.
- Slippage: 0.10% of transaction consideration.
- Base one-way friction: 0.20%.
- Double-cost stress: exchange fee 0.20% plus slippage 0.20%, or 0.40% one way.

Tax and withholding assumptions:

- Positive realized VDA gains are charged 30% income tax plus 4% cess on that tax, for an effective 31.2% estimate.
- Losing disposals receive no tax credit and cannot offset positive gains.
- The gain basis includes allocated acquisition consideration and buy-side execution costs.
- 1% TDS is withheld from every sale consideration.
- TDS withheld is removed from deployable cash and tracked separately as a receivable.
- Reports show both deployable portfolio value and economic value including the TDS receivable.
- TDS receivables cannot be reinvested during the test.

These are simplified paper-research assumptions, not personal tax advice.

## Frozen benchmarks

For the identical block and fill timestamps:

1. Cash benchmark: zero return.
2. Equal-weight six-asset buy-and-hold, entered at the activation fill hour and liquidated at the block end, using the same base costs, tax and TDS treatment.

The strategy must beat both cash and the equal-weight benchmark on deployable after-tax return.

## Discovery gate

The complete 2,160-interval discovery block passes only when all conditions hold:

- deployable after-tax net return is positive;
- economic after-tax return including TDS receivable is positive;
- deployable after-tax return is positive in both chronological halves;
- deployable after-tax return remains positive under double execution costs;
- deployable after-tax return exceeds the equal-weight benchmark;
- worst deployable-equity drawdown is no greater than 12%;
- at least 72 hourly intervals have non-zero target exposure;
- at least six distinct cash-to-position entry events occur;
- leave-one-asset-out deployable return is positive for at least four of six omissions;
- leave-one-sleeve-out deployable return is positive for at least two of three omissions; and
- no one sleeve contributes more than 70% of aggregate positive realized pre-tax gains.

Reducing losses, remaining mostly in cash or producing a positive economic value only because of TDS receivables does not pass.

## Promotion holdout gate

The holdout is evaluated once, only after discovery passes, with no parameter or accounting changes. It passes only when:

- all 720 intervals are complete;
- deployable after-tax net return is positive;
- double-cost deployable after-tax return is positive;
- deployable after-tax return exceeds cash and the equal-weight benchmark;
- worst deployable-equity drawdown is no greater than 12%; and
- at least two distinct entry events occur.

Passing authorizes only a separately reviewed, time-limited shadow-paper candidate. Continuous paper positions and real-money trading remain disabled.

## Output and integrity contract

Before discovery completion, the JSON schema contains no keys for returns, drawdown, trades, sleeve contribution or benchmark performance.

After discovery completion, the report records:

- exact forward-data branch head and every input file SHA-256;
- activation, discovery and optional holdout boundaries;
- base and double-cost accounting ledgers;
- deployable and economic after-tax equity series summaries;
- tax and TDS totals;
- entry and active-hour counts;
- cash and equal-weight benchmarks;
- chronological-half, leave-one-asset and leave-one-sleeve results;
- every frozen gate reason; and
- protocol, evaluator and router fingerprints.

Every output is canonical-hashed. Existing evaluation records cannot be replaced with different bytes.

## Stopping rule

- No threshold may be changed after activation or after seeing any discovery metric.
- No partial discovery performance may be inspected through logs, summaries or artifacts.
- A failed discovery permanently retires this exact v2.1 router on the accumulated block.
- A failed holdout permanently rejects promotion.
- A green workflow means correct execution, not profitability.

## Pre-implementation accounting clarification

Frozen before evaluator code and before any future decision is scored:

- Each discovery, stress, ablation, benchmark and holdout simulation forcibly liquidates all remaining holdings at the final block snapshot mid.
- Final liquidation receives the same sell friction, positive-gain tax and TDS treatment as any other disposal.
- An entry event is counted only when actual portfolio exposure moves from zero to positive after an hourly rebalance.
- Holdings are tracked as asset-and-sleeve lots.
- Asset reductions sell all outstanding sleeve lots proportionally by quantity and allocated basis.
- Positive taxable gain and no-loss-setoff treatment are calculated separately for each proportional lot disposal.
- Positive realized pre-tax sleeve contribution is the sum of positive lot gains before income tax, TDS and sell friction.
- The equal-weight benchmark invests one sixth of deployable starting capital in each frozen asset at the activation fill, then forcibly liquidates at the block end.

## Pre-implementation decision/fill timing clarification

Frozen before evaluator code and before any decision is scored:

- A router decision with cutoff hour `t` is first executable at the earliest valid spot mid in hour `t+1`.
- That position earns its first scored price outcome from the `t+1` fill to the `t+2` mark.
- The next decision, with cutoff `t+1`, is rebalanced at that same `t+2` mark after the prior interval is recorded.
- Therefore 2,160 scored discovery intervals require 2,160 consecutive decisions and 2,161 consecutive post-decision hourly snapshots, from activation fill through final mark.
- The final forced liquidation occurs at the last mark, after the final scored holding interval.
- The equal-weight benchmark buys at the activation fill and liquidates at the same final mark.

## Pre-implementation simulation clarification

Frozen before evaluator code and before any decision is scored:

- The accepted v2.1 router fingerprints are exactly:
  - source SHA-256 `afae56619ede5c6e459f826b6709be3b918c51a7c2fb3321d23305a0cfa17fae`;
  - protocol SHA-256 `1c77b0974f784e59e4c7d916db9dae74b5f25ad742f3fc761fca7010d2f5519d`.
- Discovery first-half, second-half, leave-one-asset and leave-one-sleeve runs are independent INR 100,000 simulations over their own frozen intervals.
- Omitted asset or sleeve target weight remains cash and is never redistributed.
- Target asset value is its router weight multiplied by deployable marked equity immediately before that hour's rebalance.
- Existing lots are not sold merely because the router's retained sleeve label changes; only quantity needed to reach the frozen asset target is traded.
- New purchases are attributed to the sleeve named by that hour's selected candidate.
- The equal-weight benchmark divides starting deployable cash into six inclusive-cost budgets; each one-sixth budget covers both consideration and buy friction.
- Taxable lot gain is sale consideration minus allocated acquisition consideration and buy-side friction. Sell friction is charged separately and is not used to reduce taxable gain.
- A missing required decision makes the frozen block incomplete. The router's operational cash fallback is not a substitute for a missing scored input record.
- Until a block is complete, no simulation helper is called and no partial performance field is written or printed.
