## Reactivation rule

A retired line may be reconsidered only when all are true:

1. the hypothesis uses genuinely new causal information rather than renamed old behavior;
2. the test interval has not been consumed by the family;
3. the permanent trial registry is updated before outcome access;
4. a finite protocol and promotion gate are frozen first;
5. the experiment remains paper-only and cannot authorize trading.
