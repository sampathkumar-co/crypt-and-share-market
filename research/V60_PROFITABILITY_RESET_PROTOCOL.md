# v6.0 Profitability Reset Protocol

Status: frozen before any new v6 profitability outcome is calculated.

## Objective

Replace the version-by-version strategy spiral with one evidence-governed champion-challenger programme. The purpose is to find materially profitable paper performance without reopening rejected families, contaminating untouched intervals, or confusing risk controls with alpha.

This protocol does not promise profit and does not authorize trading.

## Canonical roles

1. **Historical champion pending forward confirmation:** corrected v3.1.2/v3.2 BTC/ETH trend plus yielding cash.
2. **Active sealed forward programme:** v3.3, unchanged.
3. **Learned-model control:** v4.4.
4. **Frozen mechanism challenger:** v5.2 trend-acceleration recovery overlay only.
5. **Independent-feature challenger:** one turnover-safe cross-exchange confirmation design, preregistered separately before outcomes.
6. **Execution/risk library:** v5.6. It may protect an entry but may not be presented as alpha.

## Retirement policy

Rejected code and reports remain immutable research evidence. They are removed from active development by registry status and closed research pull requests, not by rewriting Git history.

A retired family may not:

- be tuned on a previously consumed interval;
- be renamed and resubmitted with economically identical behavior;
- enter a tournament without a genuinely new causal information source;
- become active because it reduces losses while remaining economically negative;
- bypass the permanent experiment and configuration count.

## Common-accounting tournament

The first v6 outcome-producing experiment must evaluate only these frozen arms:

1. yielding cash;
2. passive BTC/ETH exposure at the same maximum risk budget;
3. corrected v3.1.2/v3.2 champion;
4. v4.4 learned-model control;
5. v4.4 plus the unchanged v5.2 primary overlay.

No additional parameter grid, model family, asset, threshold, cadence, or allocation method may be added after this protocol.

All arms must use the same:

- point-in-time price and cash-yield inputs;
- next-open execution semantics;
- natural drift between scheduled rebalances;
- standard and doubled-cost assumptions;
- spread and slippage accounting;
- terminal liquidation;
- maximum total crypto exposure of 10%;
- maximum per-asset exposure of 5%, except the already frozen champion semantics where required for exact reproduction;
- reporting intervals and contribution accounting.

The tournament first reproduces each source strategy exactly. Any reproduction failure stops the tournament before comparison.

## Material profitability gate

A strategy is not a historical profitability candidate unless every condition passes:

1. annualized standard net return is at least 5%;
2. annualized net excess over yielding cash is at least 2 percentage points;
3. compounded doubled-cost return is positive;
4. at least four of five sequential untouched windows are positive under standard costs;
5. at least three of five are positive under doubled costs;
6. one-decision delayed execution remains positive;
7. maximum drawdown is no more than 5% at the frozen risk budget;
8. at least 30 independent target-changing actions occur;
9. no single trade contributes more than 20% of total positive profit;
10. no single window contributes more than 50% of total positive profit;
11. contribution is not dependent on one accidental asset or regime;
12. a moving-block or stationary bootstrap lower confidence bound is positive;
13. Deflated Sharpe probability is at least 0.95 using the complete permanent trial count;
14. CSCV Probability of Backtest Overfitting is no more than 0.20;
15. minimum track-record length is satisfied;
16. the frozen implementation reproduces on an independent price source.

A tiny positive excess, a single protective decision, or a result that merely beats a weak control is not a profitability pass.

## Forward promotion gate

The corrected v3.1.2/v3.2 champion continues through v3.3 unchanged. No interim P&L may be used for tuning.

Promotion requires the later of:

- 180 contiguous daily observations and at least eight target-changing actions for the existing v3.3 protocol; and
- any stricter future gate frozen before outcome access.

A later v6 challenger requires at least 365 sealed daily observations or 30 independent target-changing actions, whichever occurs later, unless a separate preregistered protocol justifies a longer period.

## Allowed new research after the tournament

Only two new information families are allowed initially:

1. cross-exchange confirmation that changes conviction without recreating daily unintended rebalancing;
2. derivatives positioning state used only for bounded exposure sizing, never as a direct capitulation-recovery entry.

Each family receives one finite preregistered design. Broad searches are prohibited until the permanent registry includes all historical trials and consumed intervals.

## Explicitly prohibited next moves

- v5.7 or another patch to the same tiny July overlay;
- one-hour trading based on daily alpha;
- guaranteed-profit or guaranteed-zero-loss market exposure;
- retuning rejected macro thresholds;
- adding recovery, mean-reversion, range, or neutral sleeves;
- increasing leverage or exposure to manufacture annualized return;
- using real money before sealed forward promotion.

## Safety

Paper-only. Long-or-cash. No credentials, wallets, orders, leverage, shorts, derivatives, deposits, withdrawals, lending, or staking. `authorizes_trading=false` remains mandatory.
