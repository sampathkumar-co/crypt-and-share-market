# v6.0 Profitability Reset Protocol

Status: frozen before any new v6 profitability outcome is calculated.

## Objective

Replace the version-by-version strategy spiral with one evidence-governed champion-challenger programme. The purpose is to find materially profitable paper performance without reopening rejected families, contaminating untouched intervals, or confusing risk controls with alpha.

This protocol does not promise profit and does not authorize trading.

## Canonical roles

1. Historical champion pending forward confirmation: corrected v3.1.2/v3.2 BTC/ETH trend plus yielding cash.
2. Active sealed forward programme: v3.3, unchanged.
3. Learned-model control: v4.4.
4. Frozen mechanism challenger: v5.2 trend-acceleration recovery overlay only.
5. Independent-feature challenger: one turnover-safe cross-exchange confirmation design, preregistered separately before outcomes.
6. Execution/risk library: v5.6. It may protect an entry but may not be presented as alpha.

## Retirement policy

Rejected code and reports remain immutable research evidence. They are removed from active development by registry status and closed research pull requests, not by rewriting Git history.

A retired family may not be tuned on a previously consumed interval, renamed and resubmitted with economically identical behavior, or enter a tournament without a genuinely new causal information source.

## Common-accounting tournament

The first v6 outcome-producing experiment evaluates only yielding cash, passive BTC/ETH at the same risk budget, corrected v3.1.2/v3.2, v4.4, and v4.4 plus the unchanged v5.2 primary overlay. No new parameter grid, model family, asset, threshold, cadence, or allocation method may be added.

All arms use point-in-time inputs, next-open execution, natural drift, standard and doubled costs, spread/slippage accounting, terminal liquidation, and a maximum total crypto exposure of 10%.

## Material profitability gate

A historical candidate requires annualized standard net return >= 5%, annualized excess over yielding cash >= 2 percentage points, positive doubled-cost return, four of five positive standard windows, three of five positive stress windows, positive delayed execution, maximum drawdown <= 5%, at least 30 independent target-changing actions, bounded trade/window concentration, positive block-bootstrap lower bound, DSR >= 0.95 using the complete trial count, PBO <= 0.20, sufficient track record, and independent-source replication.

A tiny positive excess or a single protective decision is not a profitability pass.

## Forward promotion

The v3.3 programme remains unchanged. Interim P&L may not be used for tuning. No result here authorizes live or continuous paper trading.

## Safety

Paper-only. Long-or-cash. No credentials, wallets, orders, leverage, shorts, derivatives, deposits, withdrawals, lending, or staking.
